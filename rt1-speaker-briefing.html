About Transport Stack

Transport Stack is an open platform of software and data for Indian urban mobility — GTFS datasets, ETA calculators, schedule adherence tools, transit APIs, and a community Wiki — custodianed by Chartr and BCG under a JICA-funded initiative with IIIT Delhi as the academic partner. The code lives on GitHub, and the platform is live at delhi.transportstack.in.

This roundtable series is the community-engagement arm of the custodianship — quarterly conversations with the ecosystem of people who generate, consume, and regulate transit data in Indian cities.

Panel Discussion: Building Open Data for India

Format: Each panelist will share one perspective (3–5 min). Then the moderator opens for conversation. The panel sets up the open discussion that follows, so the goal is to surface friction points and provocations — not to resolve everything.

Keynote: The Open Mobility Moment

Prof. Inder Gopal will give a 10–12 minute talk followed by 3–5 minutes of Q&A. The talk should cover the landscape of open data and open networks in Indian mobility — what exists, what's missing, and where the building blocks are converging.

Audio & Video Setup

  • Camera on: Please keep your camera on during your session. It makes a significant difference in an all-virtual setting.
  • Mic: A headset or external mic is preferred over your laptop's built-in mic (reduces echo).
  • Connection: A wired internet connection is recommended. If on Wi-Fi, please sit close to the router.
  • Background: A plain or blurred background works best. Or show us your bookshelf — we like those too.

What to Expect During the Event

This is not a conference. This is a focused 2-hour working conversation. There are ~20 invitees in the room, all from the Indian transit ecosystem. Expect to be in conversation, not just on stage.
  • Moderator: Prof. Pravesh Biyani will facilitate and keep time. If you're running long, don't worry — he'll give you a gentle signal.
  • Chat & Q&A: Attendees can submit questions via the Zoom Q&A panel. The moderator will read select questions aloud.
  • Recording: The session will be recorded and shared (unlisted) with all invitees and on GitHub Discussions afterwards.
  • Post-event: A summary of key takeaways will be circulated within 3 days. You'll be invited to the Transport Stack GitHub Discussions for ongoing conversation.
